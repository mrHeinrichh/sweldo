# Privacy Policy for Sweldo

**Last updated:** October 14, 2026

Sweldo is a non-custodial payroll demonstration app that operates on **Stellar Testnet**. Testnet assets have no real monetary value. This policy explains how the Sweldo Android app handles information when you use it.

## Information the app uses

- **Wallet information:** When you connect a wallet, Sweldo receives its public Stellar wallet address, network, and wallet provider. A wallet connection may also involve session identifiers and signing requests. Sweldo does not ask for or store your wallet's private key or recovery phrase; transaction approval happens in the wallet.
- **Payroll information:** You may enter or import a worker's name, public wallet address, payout amount, asset, and payout schedule. The app uses this information to prepare Testnet transactions and show payroll records. Imported roster files are read to fill the payroll form; the app does not upload the roster file to a Sweldo server. Worker names are used as labels in the app and are not intentionally included in the Stellar transactions Sweldo creates.
- **Activity and preferences on your device:** The app may save your last wallet choice, whether you have seen its guide, and recent payout claim details on your device. Some payroll details may be held in memory while the app is open. Wallet software may keep connection session data on your device.
- **Network information:** When the app connects to external services, those services may receive your IP address and technical request information as part of the connection.
- **Messages to us:** If you email the privacy contact, we receive the email address and information you include so we can respond to your inquiry.

## How information is used and shared

Sweldo uses this information to connect your wallet, show Testnet balances and payroll history, prepare and submit transactions you approve, and display payout and claim records. Sweldo does not use this information to serve ads or sell it.

To provide these features, the app communicates with:

- **Wallet providers**, including Freighter and xBull, to connect wallets and request transaction signatures. Freighter Mobile connections use the **Reown/WalletConnect** service to establish and maintain a wallet session.
- **Stellar Testnet services**, including Horizon, Soroban RPC, and, if you request test funding, Friendbot, to read public ledger information, submit signed transactions, and fund a Testnet account.
- **Stellar Expert** if you choose to open an external transaction or account explorer link.

These services are operated by third parties and may process information under their own privacy policies. Sweldo does not operate a server that stores your payroll roster or wallet private keys.

## Public blockchain records

If you approve a payroll, claim, cancellation, or other transaction, its public wallet addresses, amounts, assets, timing conditions, transaction hash, and other transaction details can become visible on the Stellar Testnet ledger and through public explorers. If the optional payroll registry is enabled, related schedule proof information can also be recorded on Testnet. **Public ledger records can be copied by others and cannot be deleted by Sweldo.** Do not enter sensitive information into a public wallet address or transaction field.

## Security

Sweldo uses encrypted network connections for its production connections to external services. Transactions must be approved through a connected wallet; Sweldo does not receive your private key or recovery phrase. Information stored on your device is subject to your device's security controls. Because Testnet ledger records are public, they should not be treated as private storage.

## Retention and deletion

Information saved by Sweldo on your device remains there until it is removed by the app, you clear the app's storage, or you uninstall the app, subject to any backup settings on your device. You can disconnect a wallet in the app. We keep privacy inquiry emails only as long as needed to handle the inquiry or meet applicable obligations. Sweldo does not offer a Sweldo account or maintain a separate account database. Clearing app data does **not** erase public Stellar Testnet records or information held independently by wallet providers and other third-party services. For privacy questions or help with data that Sweldo controls, contact us at the email address above.

## Children

Sweldo is designed for adult users of a payroll demonstration and is not directed to children.
